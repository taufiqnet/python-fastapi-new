BLA 2006 Compliant Leave & Encashment Engine

Enforcing Bangladesh statutory quotas, carryover limits, and financial encashment

Casual Leave (CL)
10 Days
Section 115

Full-pay non-cumulative leave for urgent personal affairs. Cannot combine with sick leave.

Sick Leave (SL)
14 Days
Section 116

Full-pay medical leave requiring registered doctor certificate for absences over 3 days.

Earned / Annual (EL)
1 / 18 Days
Section 117

Accrued on actual working days. Eligible for annual carryover and cash encashment.

Maternity Leave
16 Weeks
Section 46-50

8 weeks pre-natal and 8 weeks post-natal with statutory daily average wage calculation.

Festival Holidays
11 Days
Section 118

Mandatory paid national and religious festival holidays declared by government.

COMPENSATION BREAKDOWN

Earnings, Deductions & Net Salary Computation

Transparent, granular breakdown of every monetary component per payslip

Earnings Components (Gross Salary)
  • Basic Salary: 50% to 60% of Gross as per industry standard and wage gazette.
  • House Rent Allowance: 25% to 50% of Basic as per corporate compensation rules.
  • Medical & Transport: Configurable tax-free limits applied per Income Tax regulations.
  • Food & Special Allowances: Customizable recurring allowances mapped to grades.
  • Statutory Overtime: Calculated at double basic rate: (Basic ÷ 208 hrs) × 2 × OT Hours.
  • Festival Bonuses: Eid-ul-Fitr, Eid-ul-Adha, Puja, and annual performance bonuses.
Deductions & Net Disbursable
  • Withholding Tax (AIT): Automated progressive income tax deducted at source.
  • Provident Fund (RPF): 8.33% employee deduction matched with 8.33% employer contribution.
  • Unpaid Leave (LWP): Exact calendar day deduction: (Basic ÷ Working Days) × LWP.
  • Loan & Advance EMIs: Automated monthly recovery of active employee loan installments.
  • Damage & Recovery: Authorized asset damage or disciplinary penalty deductions.
  • Net Disbursable: Gross Salary − Total Deductions, automatically verified before payout.
