# Executive Management Release Brief
## ProERP SaaS Platform: HR & Payroll Modernization Program
**Release Phase:** Phase 1 — Core Workforce Foundation & Employee Directory  
**Target Release Version:** v1.1.0  
**Target Live Date:** 05 November 2026 | **Maintenance Window:** 02:00 AM – 03:30 AM BST  
**Deployment Target:** Live Production (cPanel CloudLinux)  
**Target Audience:** Board of Directors, C-Level Management, Head of Human Resources, Head of Finance  

---

### 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Objectives

The purpose of this release is to launch **Phase 1 of the ProERP HR & Payroll Modernization Initiative**. 

Historically, employee records, organizational structures, and compliance documentation have been maintained in fragmented spreadsheets and physical files. Phase 1 introduces a centralized, secure, multi-tenant digital workforce system that establishes a single source of truth for all corporate personnel.

To protect live business operations and allow HR staff to adjust comfortably, the system is being deployed using a **Controlled Incremental Rollout**:
- **Phase 1 (This Release):** Organizational structures, employee digital profiles, and statutory compliance files.
- **Phase 2 (T+14 Days):** Shift rosters, biometric time & attendance, and statutory leave management.
- **Phase 3 (T+28 Days):** Compensation structure, payroll calculation, payslips, and bank advice.
- **Phase 4 (T+42 Days):** Digital appointment/offer letters, employee appraisals, and separation settlements.

### 3. Detailed Scope: Features Delivered in Phase 1

#### 3.1 Organization & Structural Hierarchy
- **Multi-Branch Management:** Head office, regional branches, distribution centers, and retail outlets.
- **Department Taxonomy:** Hierarchical departmental structuring with cost-center attribution.
- **Standardized Job Titles:** Standard job roles and career grades eliminating designation redundancies.

#### 3.2 Master Employee Directory
- **Personal & Identity Data:** Full name, gender, date of birth, blood group, marital status, religion, National ID (NID), Passport number.
- **Employment & Job Profile:** Employee code, official email, date of joining, probation duration, confirmation date, branch, department, designation, and reporting manager.
- **Contact & Emergency Details:** Primary contact number, present and permanent addresses, emergency contact persons and relationship tags.
- **Statutory Labour Safeguards:** Mandatory compliance with the Bangladesh Labour Act 2006 (e.g. minimum age validation, employment classification: permanent, contractual, probationary, intern).

#### 3.3 Digital Personal Files (e-Dossier)
- Encrypted storage for employee documentation:
  - National ID / Smart Card copies
  - Academic certificates and professional degrees
  - Signed employment contracts & NDAs
  - Police clearance and reference letters

### 5. Risk Assessment, Mitigation & Rollback Protocol

| Risk Category | Risk Level | Mitigation Strategy | Contingency Plan |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Database Migration Interruption** | Low | Live database backup will be executed immediately before the maintenance window. | Full automated restore in under 15 minutes via `restore_database.sh`. |
| **User Data Entry Inaccuracies** | Medium | Built-in format validators; bulk import preview with error feedback. | Dedicated HR data validation clinic for the first 3 days post-release. |
| **Service Downtime Beyond Window** | Low | Maintenance window scheduled during non-business hours (02:00 AM - 03:30 AM BST). | If post-deployment diagnostic does not pass by 03:15 AM, automatic rollback to previous release tag. |
